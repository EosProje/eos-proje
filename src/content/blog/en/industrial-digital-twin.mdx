# What is a Digital Twin? The Future of Industrial Facilities

A Digital Twin is a virtual replica of a physical asset or process. However, it is not just a 3D model; it is a dynamic simulation that is constantly updated with real-time data (sensors, IoT, [laser scanning](/en/solutions/laser-scanning)).

## Industrial Use Cases

1.  **Reducing Downtime:**
    Sensor data from production lines is analyzed on the digital model to intervene before failures occur.
2.  **Training and Simulation:**
    Hazardous operations or fire drills can be safely conducted in a virtual environment (VR/XR).
3.  **Asset Management:**
    The maintenance history of every valve, pump, or pipe is stored in tags on the model.

## How Is It Created?

A Digital Twin consists of **static model** and **dynamic data** layers. This means that the millimetric geometry of the facility must first be obtained through laser scanning (static), and then SCADA systems must be integrated.

- **Laser Scanning:** EosProje scans your facility to create the "base geometry."
- **BIM Modeling:** This geometry is transformed into intelligent objects through the [Point Cloud to BIM](/en/solutions/point-cloud-to-bim) process.
- **Integration:** IoT data is connected to the resulting model.

## Efficiency Gains in [Industrial Facilities](/en/sectors/industrial-facilities)

Searching for a valve in a complex petrochemical facility can take hours, while it can be found in seconds on a digital twin. This increases operational efficiency by 20-30%.
