# What is Scan to BIM?

It is the process of converting an existing building, bridge, or facility into a 3D Building Information Model (BIM) using [laser scanning](/en/solutions/laser-scanning) ([point cloud](/en/solutions/point-cloud-to-bim)) data. This model is not just a visual representation; it is an intelligent database containing the geometric, technical, and quantity information of the structure.

## The 5-Step Process

The Scan to BIM process begins with fieldwork and ends in the office environment.

1.  **Field Planning (Survey Plan):** Site reconnaissance and determination of scanner positions.
2.  **Data Collection (Laser Scanning):** Point cloud data collection with LiDAR devices like Leica RTC360.
3.  **Registration:** Merging fragmented scans into a single coordinate system (Autodesk ReCap / Cyclone).
4.  **Modeling (BIM Production):** Modeling architectural, structural, and MEP elements [in Revit](/en/blog/point-cloud-to-bim-process) by tracing over the point cloud.
5.  **Quality Control (QA/QC):** Analyzing deviations between the model and the point cloud (Navisworks / Verity).

## Why Is It Necessary?

In renovation projects, drawings of the existing structure are often lost or outdated (walls demolished, installations changed). With Scan to BIM, the "as-built" condition is documented with 100% accuracy.

- **Risk Management:** [Clashes are detected](/en/solutions/clash-detection) at the design stage (Clash Detection).
- **Cost Control:** The most accurate quantity lists are obtained for manufacturing.
- **Facility Management:** An up-to-date [digital twin](/en/sectors/industrial-facilities) is provided for maintenance and repair processes.
