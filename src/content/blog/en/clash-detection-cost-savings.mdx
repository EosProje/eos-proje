# Clash Detection: Debugging in the Virtual Construction Site

One of the biggest cost items in construction projects is "Rework" costs. Discovering on-site that a ventilation duct collides with a steel beam causes work stoppage, material waste, and increased labor costs. BIM (Building Information Modeling) technology fundamentally solves this problem with **[Clash Detection](/en/solutions/point-cloud-to-bim)**.

## Types of Clashes

We can discuss three types of clashes in a project:

1.  **Hard Clash:** The physical intersection of two building elements.
    *   *Example:* A fire pipe passing through a column.
2.  **Soft Clash (Clearance):** Violation of the required space for assembly or maintenance of an element.
    *   *Example:* Insufficient distance in front of a valve for maintenance.
3.  **Workflow Clash:** Timing errors during construction.
    *   *Example:* Trying to install pipes after a wall has been closed.

## Cost Savings Analysis

Research (Penn State BIM Guide) shows that the cost of a clash increases logarithmically depending on which phase of the project it is detected.

| Detection Phase | Solution Cost (Estimate) | Impact Duration |
|----------------|--------------------------|----------------|
| **Design (BIM)** | $20 - $50 (Engineer Hours) | 1 Hour |
| **Tender / Procurement** | $500 - $1,000 (Revision) | 1 Day |
| **Site (During Construction)** | $5,000+ (Removal, Materials, Delays) | 3-7 Days |

<Callout type="summary">
**Example Scenario:** In a 50,000 m² industrial facility project, **1,500 hard clashes** were detected and resolved at the design table through BIM coordination. If these errors had reached the site, they would have caused an average of 5%-10% deviation in the project budget.
</Callout>
