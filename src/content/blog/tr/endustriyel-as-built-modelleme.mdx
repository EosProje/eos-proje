# Endüstriyel Tesislerde As-Built Modelleme

<Callout type="summary">
**TL;DR:** [Endüstriyel tesislerde](/sektorler/endustriyel-tesis) (fabrika, rafineri, enerji santrali) as-built BIM modelleme, standart binalardan %300 daha karmaşıktır: Yoğun MEP sistemleri (pipe density 15-25 m/m²), active production ortamında çalışma, safety protocols. [Toyota Fabrikası](/referanslar/toyota-fabrikasi) projemizde 50,000 m² tesiste 2,500+ MEP element, LOD 300, 8 hafta. Başarı anahtarı: Detaylı planlama + experienced team + phased approach.
</Callout>

## Endüstriyel Tesislerin Özgün Zorlukları

### 1. M EP Sistemleri Yoğunluğu

**Standart Ofis Binası:** 3-5 metre boru/m²  
**Endüstriyel Tesis:** 15-25 metre boru/m² (5-8x daha yoğun!)

Toyota projemizde sadece process area'da:
- 800+ boru hattı (DN25-DN800)
- 350+ vana
- 150+ equipment (tanks, pumps, heat exchangers)
- 200+ instrumentation points

<Callout type="warning">
**Pipe Complexity:** Bir process unit'te 6 farklı sistem bir arada: Process fluid, cooling water, steam, compressed air, chemical drain, fire fighting. Her biri farklı materyal, pressure rating, insulation requirement.
</Callout>

---

### 2. Active Production Ortamında Çalışma

Fabrikalar 7/24 üretim yapıyor - laser scanning nasıl yapılır?

#### Stratejiler:

**A. Vardiya Saatleri Dışı:**
- Gece 00:00-06:00 arası
- Production downtime minimize
- Safety risk azaltma

**B. Modüler Approach:**
- Fabrika zone by zone taranır
- Critical production areas weekend'de
- Non-critical areas normal saatlerde

**C. Remote Scanning:**
- Tehlikeli alanlarda (yüksek sıcaklık, kimyasal risk)
- Drone-based scanning (outdoor areas)

---

### 3. Safety Protocols

Endüstriyel tesislerde safety paramount:

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| HSE Training | Minimum 8 saat site-specific |
| PPE | Helmet, safety shoes, goggles, earplugs |
| Gas Detection | Personal H2S, LEL detectors |
| Permits | Work permit, hot work permit (if required) |
| Escorts | Production team escort mandatory |

## Endüstriyel As-Built Modelleme Süreci

### Faz 1: Pre-Project Planning (1-2 hafta)

- Client briefing + site visit
- Existing documentation review (P&IDs, isometrics)
- Safety training
- Scan planning (zone division)
- **Deliverable:** Project Execution Plan (PEP)

---

### Faz 2: Laser Scanning (3-7 gün)

**Scan Density Requirements:**
- Process piping areas: 3-5mm
- Structural steel: 5-10mm
- General areas: 10-15mm

**Toyota Project Stats:**
- 250+ scan positions
- 45 TB raw data
- 5 billion points
- Accuracy: ±3mm global registration

---

### Faz 3: Point Cloud Processing (1 hafta)

Endüstriyel tesislerde unique challenges:

**Hot Equipment:** Lazer beamlar hot surfaces'den (300°C+) scatter eder → noise  
**Çözüm:** Shutdown planlarken değerlendirin veya cold areas öncelik

**Reflective Surfaces:** Stainless steel tanks, polished pipes → multiple reflections  
**Çözüm:** Target-based registration + overlap increase

**Vibration:** Rotating equipment vibration → blurred points  
**Çözüm:** Equipment shutdown veya multiple scans average

---

### Faz 4: BIM Modelleme (4-6 hafta)

#### Modelleme Öncelikleri:

**Week 1-2: Structural & Equipment**
- Steel structure (columns, beams, platforms)
- Major equipment (tanks, vessels, pumps)
- Equipment foundations

**Week 3-4: Piping Systems**
- Main process lines (critical diameters DN150+)
- Headers and risers
- Pipe supports

**Week 5-6: Secondary Systems & Details**
- Small bore piping (DN50 altı)
- Instrumentation
- Cable ways
- Fire fighting system

---

### Faz 5: QA/QC (1 hafta)

**Critical Checks:**
- [ ] Pipe connectivity (inlet/outlet match)
- [ ] Elevation verification (±10mm)
- [ ] Flange-to-flange dimensions
- [ ] Equipment nozzle orientations
- [ ] Clash detection (hard clashes = 0)
- [ ] P&ID reconciliation

<ExpertQuote author="Uğur Bilen" role="Eos Proje">
Polyol Thyssenkrupp projesinde 450+ pipe connections'ı P&ID ile reconcile ettik. 12 discrepancy bulduk - field verification sonrası P&ID updated etti. BIM model, P&ID hatası yakaladı.
</ExpertQuote>

## Sector-Specific Considerations

### Petrokimya / Rafineri

**Özellikler:**
- High pressure/temperature lines
- Exotic materials (Hastelloy, Inconel)
- Complex instrumentation
- Flare systems

**LOD:** 350-400 (fabrication required)

---

### Enerji Santrali (Linde Tiszaujvaros)

**Özellikler:**
- Large diameter piping (DN600-DN1200)
- Insulation modeling (100-200mm thick)
- Cable tray networks
- HVAC systems (büyük duct sizes)

**LOD:** 300-350

---

### Üretim Tesisi (Toyota, Şişecam)

**Özellikler:**
- Process equipment chains
- Conveyor systems
- Utility distribution
- Clean room requirements (varsa)

**LOD:** 300

## Sıkça Sorulan Sorular

<FAQSection>

<FAQ question="Endüstriyel tesis as-built modelleme normal binadan ne kadar pahalıdır?">
**%150-250 daha pahalıdır** çünkü:
- MEP density 5-8x fazla
- Safety requirements & training
- Complex geometry (pipe routing)
- Specialized expertise (process knowledge)
- QA/QC more intensive

m² başına maliyet: 45-75 TL/m² (LOD 300, MEP dahil) vs. ofis binası 25-35 TL/m²
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="Active production devam ederken tarama yapılabilir mi?">
Evet, mümkündür ancak:
- HSE protocols strict compliance
- Production team coordination critical
- Phased approach (zone by zone)
- Night shifts veya weekend çalışma
- Some areas shutdown gerektirebilir

Toyota projesinde %80 areas active production altında tarandı, %20 planned shutdown.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="Pipe routing accuracy ne olmalı?">
LOD 300 için tolerance levels:
- **Horizontal position:** ±10-15mm
- **Elevation:** ±10mm
- **Pipe diameter:** Nominal (accurate)
- **Flange-to-flange:** ±5mm

Critical tie-in points (existing+new) için ±5mm recommended.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="P&ID reconciliation nedir, neden gerekli?">
**P&ID (Piping & Instrumentation Diagram):** Process flow şeması

**Reconciliation:** BIM model ile P&ID'yi karşılaştırma
- As-built gerçekten P&ID'ye uygun mu?
- Modifications capture edilmiş mi?
- Instrumentation tam mı?

**Neden Önemli:** P&ID, operational critical document. As-built BIM ile sync olmalı. Bizim projelerde %5-10 discrepancy bulunuyor (genellikle P&ID outdated).
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="Hangi BIM yazılımları endüstriyel tesisler için uygundur?">
| Yazılım | Best For | Strengths |
|---------|----------|-----------|
| **Revit** | General industrial | Yaygın, IFC export |
| **AutoCAD Plant 3D** | Process plants | P&ID integration |
| **PDMS/E3D** | Large refineries | Advanced piping tools |
| **Navisworks** | Coordination | Clash detection |

Eos Proje uses: **Revit (modeling) + Navisworks (QA/QC)** combo.
</FAQ>

</FAQSection>

## Best Practices: Endüstriyel As-Built Modelleme

1. **Detailed scan planning** - Zone mapping, safety briefing
2. **Phased scanning** - Minimize production impact
3. **High overlap** - %40-50 overlap (vs. %30 normal)
4. **P&ID as reference** - Process understanding critical
5. **Modular modeling** - Workset strategy for large data
6. **Regular client review** - Weekly progress check
7. **Safety documentation** - Risk assessment, work permits
8. **Specialized team** - Industrial experience mandatory
9. **Equipment data sheets** - Vendor data integration
10. **Flexible timeline** - Production delays happen, plan buffer
