# Heritage BIM for Restoration

Historical buildings require specialized documentation approaches. With [laser scanning](/en/solutions/point-cloud-to-bim) and HBIM expertise, we preserve architectural heritage digitally.

## Our Heritage Services

- **Detailed Survey:** Capturing ornate details, reliefs, and decorations.
- **Damage Assessment:** Documenting cracks, deformations, and deterioration.
- **HBIM Modeling:** Creating semantically rich heritage models.
- **Archival Documentation:** Long-term digital preservation.

## Why HBIM?

1. **Preservation:** Digital record for future generations.
2. **Planning:** Accurate data for restoration planning.
3. **Monitoring:** Baseline for structural health monitoring.
4. **Research:** Data for architectural historians and researchers.

## Notable Projects

We have documented numerous heritage sites including Ottoman-era buildings, historical mosques, and protected monuments.
