# BIM Solutions for Construction Sector

Construction projects require accurate documentation at every stage. With [laser scanning](/en/solutions/point-cloud-to-bim) and BIM modeling services, we minimize errors on-site and ensure projects are completed on time.

## Our Services

- **As-Built Documentation:** Recording existing structures with millimeter precision.
- **Clash Detection:** Early detection of conflicts between disciplines.
- **4D BIM:** Project progress tracking with time-based models.
- **Quantity Takeoff:** Accurate quantity extraction from BIM models.

## Benefits

1. **Cost Savings:** Detection of potential clashes before on-site work.
2. **Time Efficiency:** Acceleration of measurement and documentation processes.
3. **Quality Control:** Continuous tracking of project progress.
