# Architectural BIM and Documentation Services

Architects need accurate base data for renovation, renovation and new design projects. With [3D laser scanning](/en/solutions/point-cloud-to-bim) technology, we provide measurements with millimeter precision.

## Our Capabilities

- **Facade Survey:** Precise documentation of all facade details.
- **Interior Space Scanning:** Furniture and fixture-included 3D models.
- **Historical Building Documentation:** Detailed as-built for restoration projects.
- **Design Support:** BIM models suitable for Revit workflow.

## Working Process

1. **Site Survey:** Establishing scanning strategy based on project requirements.
2. **Data Collection:** Comprehensive scanning with laser scanners.
3. **Processing:** Point cloud processing and noise cleaning.
4. **Modeling:** Creating BIM model at the requested LOD level.
5. **Delivery:** Delivery in format of your choice (RVT, DWG, IFC, etc.).
