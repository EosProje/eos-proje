# Renovation Projects with BIM

Renovation projects start with accurate information about existing conditions. With [laser scanning](/en/solutions/point-cloud-to-bim), we eliminate guesswork and provide designers with precise data.

## Why Choose Us for Renovation?

- **Accurate Baseline:** ±2mm precision as-built documentation.
- **Complete Coverage:** Walls, floors, ceilings, MEP systems all captured.
- **Design-Ready Output:** Models formatted for immediate design use.
- **Clash Prevention:** Early detection of potential issues.

## Project Types

1. **Retail Store Renovations:** Shopping malls, supermarkets, boutiques.
2. **Office Fit-Outs:** Corporate spaces, co-working areas.
3. **Residential Upgrades:** Apartments, villas, housing complexes.
4. **Industrial Renovations:** Factory upgrades, warehouse conversions.
