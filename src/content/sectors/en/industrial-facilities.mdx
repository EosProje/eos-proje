# Digital Transformation in Industrial Facilities

In facilities with complex piping systems, tight mechanical rooms, and hard-to-reach equipment, traditional measurement methods fall short. With [laser scanning technology](/en/solutions/point-cloud-to-bim), we digitize your facility with millimeter precision.

## Why Laser Scanning?

- **Precision:** Capturing all geometry with ±2mm accuracy.
- **Speed:** Data collection 10 times faster than traditional methods.
- **Safety:** Remote measurement capability without entering hazardous areas.
- **Integration:** Clash detection between existing conditions (As-built) and new design ([BIM](/en/solutions/point-cloud-to-bim)).

To create a digital twin for your industrial facility projects, [contact us](/en/contact).

## Use Cases

1. **Revision Projects:** Current condition analysis in new equipment or line addition processes.
2. **Maintenance & Repair:** Asset management and digital twin creation.
3. **Assembly Simulation:** Analysis of entry paths for large parts into the facility.
