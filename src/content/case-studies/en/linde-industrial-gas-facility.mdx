# Linde Industrial Gas Facility: Boiler Room As-Built BIM

## Project Summary

This [as-built BIM modeling](/en/solutions/as-built-modeling) project was carried out in collaboration with Teknokon engineering firm at the Linde industrial gas production facility in Hungary. The objective was to measure the high-pressure boiler room and connected piping systems using laser scanning and produce an [MEP model](/en/solutions/industrial-equipment-modeling) at LOD 300 level.

## Project Scope

Linde wanted to integrate a new production line into their existing facility. For this, they needed to know the exact locations and dimensions of existing MEP systems (piping, pressure vessels, heat exchangers).

**Key Challenges:**
1. **High Pressure Environment:** Requirement to work during active production
2. **Complex Piping:** Multiple pipe lines from DN25 to DN500
3. **International Standards:** Compliance with German DIN and European EN norms

---

## Technical Approach

### Laser Scanning Process

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Equipment Used** | Leica RTC360 |
| **Scan Positions** | 180+ |
| **Accuracy** | ±3mm |
| **Coverage Area** | Boiler room + surrounding piping |
| **Site Duration** | 4 Days |

### MEP Modeling

The following systems were modeled in Autodesk Revit from point cloud data:

- **Process Piping:** High-pressure gas lines, cooling water circuits
- **Pressure Vessels:** Boiler, separator, and buffer tanks
- **Heat Exchangers:** Including dimensions and connection points
- **Safety Systems:** Safety valves, manometer points

**International Collaboration:** This project was conducted through Eos Proje's Budapest office and represents a concrete example of our industrial BIM expertise in Central Europe.

---

## Project Deliverables

- ✅ **Revit MEP Model (.rvt)** — LOD 300, complete piping and equipment
- ✅ **P&ID Verification Report** — As-built vs. design comparison
- ✅ **Piping Inventory List** — Excel format, diameter/material/length
- ✅ **IFC 2x3 Export** — For coordination and delivery
- ✅ **QA/QC Deviation Report** — ±8mm average accuracy certificate
