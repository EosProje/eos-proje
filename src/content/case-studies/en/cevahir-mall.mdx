# Cevahir Mall: Commercial Space Verification and Renovation Base

## Project Summary

Before the comprehensive renovation planned at Cevahir Mall, one of Istanbul's busiest shopping centers, we documented the current state of 25,000 m² of retail and common areas. The work was carried out using laser scanning during night shifts, and a [LOD 300 BIM model](/en/solutions/point-cloud-to-bim) including [MEP (visible installations)](/en/solutions/industrial-equipment-modeling) disciplines was created from the data.

## Client Requirements

Mall management determined that existing project drawings had become outdated due to renovations over the years. For new leasing strategies and renovation project, they needed:

1. **Accurate Square Footage:** Precise measurement and verification of leasable areas (GLA).
2. **Ceiling Coordination:** Detection of installation routes above and below the suspended ceiling.
3. **Fast Delivery:** Data collection without disrupting mall operations.

---

## Operational Process

Due to the mall being open between 10:00 - 22:00, all site work was planned between **23:00 - 05:00**.

### Site Scanning Statistics

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Total Area** | ~25,000 m² |
| **Scanning Stations** | 450+ |
| **Site Duration** | 10 Nights |
| **Equipment Used** | Leica RTC360 & BLK2GO (Mobile Scanner) |

* **Static Scanning:** For main corridors, atrium, and store fronts requiring detail.
* **Mobile Scanning:** For service corridors, fire stairs, and parking connections (speed-focused).

---

## Data Analysis and Area Calculations

Area calculations compliant with **BOMA (Building Owners and Managers Association)** standards were made on the BIM model and compared with existing lease contracts.

### Area Deviation Table (Sample Block)

| Section | Current Contract (m²) | Laser Scanning (m²) | Difference | Explanation |
|---------|----------------------|---------------------|------------|-------------|
| Store A-102 | 150.00 | 148.50 | -1.50 | Column cladding difference |
| Store B-205 | 85.00 | 92.40 | +7.40 | Storage area not included |
| Common Area (Corridor) | 1200.00 | 1195.20 | -4.80 | - |

**ROI (Return on Investment):** As a result of precise measurements, a potential increase of **3.5%** was identified in billable leasable areas. This difference enabled the project cost to be amortized within 2 months.

---

## Technical Modeling Details

The model is not just walls and floors; it contains critical components to form the base for renovation architects:

* **Suspended Ceiling Grid:** Exact processing of existing tile layout (critical for lighting placement).
* **Fire Cabinets & Sprinklers:** Location detection for preservation of existing lines.
* **Floor Levels:** Slopes and level differences in corridors modeled as "Toposurface" or "Sloped Floor".

## Conclusion

Eos Proje delivered the reliable, up-to-date, and three-dimensional digital base needed by mall management and the renovation team within just 3 weeks. This project became the first step in the mall's digital transformation process.
