# Migros Store Chain: Multi-Location As-Built BIM Modeling

## Project Summary

As part of Migros Ticaret A.S.'s renovation program, this project involved documenting the current state of multiple stores across Istanbul using [laser scanning](/en/solutions/point-cloud-to-bim) and producing [Revit BIM models](/en/solutions/point-cloud-to-bim) at LOD 300 level. Scanning performed during night shifts while stores remained operational provided error-free digital bases for renovation architects.

## Project Background

Migros needed accurate digital records of existing store geometries to accelerate and standardize their store renovation program. Traditional manual measurement methods:

- Caused **time loss** (3-5 days per store)
- Increased **error risk** (±50mm tolerance)
- Failed to capture **ceiling and installation** information

Eos Proje's laser scanning solution eliminated these problems.

## Operational Process

Due to stores being open between 08:00-22:00, all scanning work was planned between **22:30-05:30**.

## BIM Modeling Scope

The LOD 300 Revit model produced for each store includes:

- **Architectural:** Walls, floors, ceilings, door/window openings
- **MEP (Visible):** Sprinkler lines, HVAC ducts, electrical cable trays
- **Furniture Base:** Space reservation for shelf systems and checkout islands
- **Area Calculations:** Net/gross area verification per BOMA standard

**Result:** As-built models produced with laser scanning reduced design-implementation discrepancies in renovation projects by **85%**. An average of **2 weeks** design time was saved for each store.
