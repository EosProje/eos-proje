# Turkcell Store Chain: Rapid As-Built Survey Program

## Project Summary

As part of Turkcell's store renovation and standardization program, this project involved as-built documentation and survey production of multiple store locations in Istanbul using laser scanning. Scanning performed with minimum disruption at operational stores provided fast and reliable measurement data for renovation teams.

## Project Requirements

Turkcell needed the existing geometry of each location to accelerate their store renovation program. Traditional measurement methods were inadequate because:

- 1-2 day manual measurement process for each store
- Inaccurate design bases with ±20-30mm tolerance
- Missing ceiling and installation information

---

## Rapid Delivery Model

A special **"Rapid Survey"** workflow was developed for the Turkcell project:

| Phase | Duration | Output |
|-------|----------|--------|
| **Site Scanning** | 2-3 hours / store | Raw point cloud |
| **Data Processing** | 1 business day | Cleaned point cloud |
| **Survey Production** | 2 business days | As-built plans + BIM model |
| **Total** | **3-4 business days** | Delivery-ready package |

**Comparison:** With traditional manual measurement, the same store required 5-7 business days and ±30mm margin of error. With laser scanning, 3-4 business days and ±3mm accuracy.

---

## Technical Deliverables

- ✅ **As-Built Floor Plans (.dwg / .pdf)** — 1/50 scale
- ✅ **Ceiling Plans (RCP)** — Lighting and installation locations
- ✅ **LOD 200 Revit Model** — For design base
- ✅ **Area Table** — Net usable area verification
