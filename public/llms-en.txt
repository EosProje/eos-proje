## Company Identity

EOS Proje is a specialist engineering firm that converts LiDAR-based point cloud data into high-accuracy BIM models, 2D as-built drawings, and 3D digital twins. Operating from offices in Istanbul and Budapest (partner office), EOS Proje serves clients across Turkey and Europe.

Target clients: Architects, structural and MEP engineers, facility managers, EPC contractors, heritage restoration specialists, and industrial facility operators.

---

## Services

### 1. Point Cloud to BIM (Scan to BIM)

Converting point cloud data — captured via laser scanning or photogrammetry — into fully coordinated BIM models inside Autodesk Revit.

**Details:**
- Supported LOD levels: LOD 100, LOD 200, LOD 300, LOD 350, LOD 400
- Model disciplines: Architectural, Structural, MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing)
- Use cases: Renovation and retrofit projects, as-built verification, permit applications, project coordination, design validation
- Typical accuracy range: ±2mm to ±5mm

**Workflow:**
1. Client submits point cloud data (.E57, .RCP, .RCS, .LAS, .LAZ, .PTX)
2. Data quality check and coordinate system alignment
3. Revit modelling according to agreed LOD and disciplines
4. Quality control and deviation analysis
5. Delivery: .RVT, .IFC, .DWG, .NWD, 3D PDF

---

### 2. Industrial Facility & MEP Modelling

3D modelling of factories, refineries, power plants, and other industrial facilities from point cloud data.

**Scope:**
- Piping and process equipment modelling
- HVAC systems modelling
- Steel structure and structural frame modelling
- Clash detection — multi-discipline clash analysis via Navisworks
- Output in Revit or AutoCAD Plant 3D

**Use cases:** Facility modernisation, expansion projects, operational risk reduction, existing facility documentation.

---

### 3. Heritage & Architectural Survey (3D Laser Scanning)

Millimetre-accurate measurement and documentation of cultural heritage buildings, historic structures, and archaeological sites.

**Scope:**
- Field scanning with Leica and Trimble 3D laser scanners
- Point cloud processing and cleaning (registration, noise filtering)
- Production of architectural survey drawings: floor plans, sections, elevations (.DWG)
- 3D modelling for restoration and listed building applications
- Deformation and structural deviation analysis

**Accuracy:** ±1mm to ±3mm field accuracy depending on scanner and conditions.

---

### 4. As-Built Modelling & Digital Twin

Capturing the real-world state (as-is) of existing buildings and infrastructure and making it usable for Facility Management purposes.

**Scope:**
- As-built BIM model production (including deviation analysis vs. original design)
- COBie-compliant data output for facility management systems
- Digital twin infrastructure ready for IoT integration
- Export support for IWMS / CAFM systems

**Benefits:** Reduced maintenance costs, energy efficiency analysis, emergency planning, space management optimisation.

## Technical Capacity

### Accepted Input Formats
| Format | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| .E57 | Standard point cloud archive format |
| .RCP / .RCS | Autodesk ReCap project and scan files |
| .LAS / .LAZ | LiDAR standard format (uncompressed / compressed) |
| .PTX | Leica scanner raw output format |

### Delivered Output Formats
| Format | Use |
|--------|-----|
| .RVT | Autodesk Revit model |
| .IFC | Open BIM standard, software-agnostic |
| .DWG | AutoCAD 2D/3D drawing |
| .NWD | Navisworks coordination and clash model |
| 3D PDF | Visualisation and client presentation |

### Hardware & Software
- **Scanners:** Leica RTC360, Leica BLK360, Trimble X7
- **Software:** Autodesk Revit, Autodesk ReCap, Navisworks Manage, AutoCAD, AutoCAD Plant 3D

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How do I submit my point cloud data?**
A: Large files can be transferred securely via WeTransfer, Google Drive, or FTP. Please contact info@eosproje.com to initiate data transfer.

**Q: Which LOD level should I choose?**
A: It depends on your intended use. LOD 200–300 is suitable for coordination and spatial verification. LOD 350–400 is appropriate for contractor-level construction detail. EOS Proje can evaluate your project and recommend the right level.

**Q: Can I get only 2D drawings without a 3D model?**
A: Yes. Floor plans, sections, and elevations in .DWG format can be produced without a full 3D model.

**Q: Do you also provide laser scanning services on-site?**
A: Yes. On-site scanning services using Leica and Trimble scanners are available across Turkey. For international projects, coordination is handled through our Budapest partner office.

**Q: What is the typical project turnaround time?**
A: It depends on the project size and LOD level. Small projects (single floor, under 500 m²) are typically delivered within 5–10 business days. A detailed schedule is provided for larger industrial projects.

**Q: Can I receive an IFC file?**
A: Yes. IFC output is available as standard on all projects.

**Q: Do you work with international clients?**
A: Yes. EOS Proje regularly works with clients across Europe. Our Budapest partner office (Intera Survey) supports European project coordination and on-site scanning needs.

**Q: What coordinate systems do you support?**
A: We work with all standard coordinate systems including WGS84, ETRS89, local national grids, and project-specific coordinate systems as required.
