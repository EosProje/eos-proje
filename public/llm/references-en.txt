### Toyota Factory — Industrial Facility Scan to BIM
**Location:** Sakarya, Turkey | **Year:** 2024 | **Area:** 50,000 m² | **LOD:** LOD 300
**Sector:** Automotive / Industrial | **Service:** Point Cloud to BIM, MEP Modelling, Clash Detection

LOD 300 structural and MEP modelling across 50,000 m² of Toyota production facility at ±8mm accuracy. Navisworks clash analysis between new line design and existing facility. Assembly conflicts identified at design stage, delivering approximately 20% cost savings. Zero on-site revisions.
